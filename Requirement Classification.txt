 Requirement Classification

| ID | Requirement | Type | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | A patient can view the days and times when a doctor is available. | Functional | Functionality |
| R2 | A patient can book an appointment during an available time slot. | Functional | Functionality |
| R3 | A doctor can accept or decline an appointment request. | Functional | Functionality |
| R4 | A patient can have at most 3 upcoming appointments at any given time. | Functional | Business Rule / Constraint |
| R5 | Patient medical data shall be accessible only to authorized staff. | Non-functional | Security |
| R6 | Available appointment times shall be displayed within 2 seconds. | Non-functional | Performance |
| R7 | The application shall be usable on mobile phone screens. | Non-functional | Usability / Compatibility |
